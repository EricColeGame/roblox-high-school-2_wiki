## FAQ

**Is the Roblox High School 2 basement key tradeable?**
Community reports are mixed. If the key exists in your inventory as a named item, it may be tradeable depending on the current rules for the game. If it's a session unlock rather than an item, it can't be traded at all. Check your inventory screen to see which situation you're in before offering it to anyone.

**Does the key work in every server?**
Not necessarily. If the key is tied to a session or to progress you've made in a specific server, joining a new one can reset your access. Many players recommend completing the unlock and using it in the same sitting.

**What should I do if the basement door still won't open?**
First, confirm the key is actually in your inventory and try activating it from there. Second, check that you're at the correct door — Roblox High School 2 has more than one locked area. Third, ask in chat. If several players report the same problem, it's likely a temporary issue rather than something you're doing wrong.

**Do I need the basement key for anything besides the basement?**
Generally, no. It's a utility item tied to a specific area. Don't spend a lot of time or in-game resources chasing it if your only goal is character progression — the value here is exploration and roleplay, not power.

Once you're through the door, take a minute to look around. The basement is one of those corners of Roblox High School 2 that rewards curiosity more than efficiency, and that's exactly why so many players keep coming back to it.
