## How to Spot Fake "Free Items" Offers

This is the part nobody likes talking about, but it matters more than any grind. Searching for roblox high school 2 free items will surface a flood of websites, videos, and Discord posts promising instant rewards — and almost none of them are real.

| Red Flag | Why It's a Scam |
|---|---|
| "Free Robux generator" sites | No such tool exists; they harvest logins |
| Anyone asking for your password or 2FA code | Legitimate players and staff never need it |
| Off-platform login pages | Classic phishing; always stay on the official Roblox domain |
| "Code lists" with no official source | Community reports consistently flag these as bait |
| Promises of unlimited currency | Currencies are server-side and cannot be injected |

For a plain-language rundown of what does and doesn't exist, Roblox's official help center is the safest place to check before you click anything. The rule is simple: if a site asks you to log in somewhere other than the official Roblox domain, close the tab.
