## How the Graduation System Works in Roblox High School 2

Roblox High School 2, developed by Cinder Studio, is a social roleplay experience rather than a linear story game. Graduation is therefore not tied to a single quest chain. It is tied to a progress threshold that you reach by banking XP, and the classroom is your main engine. Every period you attend gives you a shot at XP and a grade, and both feed your standing in the school.

Because the experience has been updated many times since launch, the exact graduation requirement has shifted, and community reports do not always agree on the current number. Rather than trusting an old screenshot or a random comment, open your own profile in-game and watch the progress bar. That bar is the only number that applies to your account.

## Step-by-Step: How to Graduate in Roblox High School 2

If you want a repeatable routine that moves your progress bar every single session, follow this order. None of these steps require Robux, and all of them work whether you play for twenty minutes or two hours.

1. **Learn the bell schedule.** Classes run on a timer. Knowing when each period starts means you are never sprinting down a hallway while the door closes behind you.
2. **Arrive early and stay put.** Walking in before the bell and staying in your seat for the full period is the single most reliable way to earn credit.
3. **Treat minigames like real tests.** Accuracy beats frantic clicking. Players who slow down and read the prompts usually post better scores and better grades.
4. **Do homework between periods.** Homework is fast, repeatable, and easy to forget. It is one of the best quiet sources of progress in the game.
5. **Take a part-time job.** Jobs around town fund your outfits and give you something to do during gaps between classes. Some also feed a little XP into your account.
6. **Show up to school events.** Proms and seasonal events are social, fun, and — according to community reports — can hand out bonus XP for attending.
7. **Check your progress bar after every session.** Tracking your bar turns a vague grind into a visible finish line.
