## Progression Tips for New and Returning Students

Whether you started in 2018 or installed the game yesterday, a few habits make the early grind smoother.

- Pick a routine early. Alternating between class time and free roam prevents burnout.
- Spend your first rewards on avatar items you actually like. Identification matters in a social sim.
- Claim a home before you start hoarding furniture. Storage without a base gets messy fast.
- Join servers at peak hours. With 30-player lobbies, an off-peak server can feel empty.
- Revisit the official page after big updates. Patch notes and new store items usually land there first.
- Do not rush the social side. Long-term friends, not currency, keep most players coming back.
