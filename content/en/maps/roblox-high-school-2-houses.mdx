## Why Houses Matter in Roblox High School 2

A house isn't just decoration. It's your spawn point, your hangout spot, and your personal showroom. Unlike a new outfit that only you can see from the right camera angle, a home is a space other players can walk through, judge, and compliment.

That makes housing one of the few purchases in Roblox High School 2 that keeps paying off. You can invite friends over between classes, park your avatar somewhere quiet to plan your next grind, and slowly fill rooms with furniture as your budget grows.

Community reports also suggest that homes function as a soft progress tracker. When a player is rolling around in a Modern 2 Story House, you can reasonably guess they've put serious hours into classes, jobs, and currency grinding. For many players, the house list has become an informal scoreboard.

The catch: prices scale aggressively. The jump from a starter build to a mid-tier home is steep, and the gem-priced houses sit in a completely different economy. Knowing those numbers ahead of time keeps you from wasting a week of grinding on the wrong property.

If you want the single smartest purchase on the board, community sentiment usually lands on the Ranch House. ## How to Earn Currency Faster for Your Next Home

Housing prices only make sense next to income. Based on player experience, here's where most of your money comes from in Roblox High School 2.

| Activity | Currency Earned | Best For | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Attending classes | Coins | Steady baseline | Reliable, low effort, stacks over sessions |
| Working jobs | Coins | Bigger hourly totals | Player reports suggest longer shifts pay better |
| General gameplay and events | Coins | Passive top-ups | Adds up while you're doing other things |
| Rare reward sources | Gems | Long-term saving | Gem income is slow — start early |

A few habits that separate fast savers from stuck ones:

- **Pick a target house and reverse-engineer it.** Need 9,000 coins? Break it into daily chunks instead of staring at the full number.
- **Don't buy furniture until you buy the house.** Decorating a starter you're about to leave is wasted spending.
- **Treat gems as untouchable.** Since only two homes use gems, spending them on anything else delays the Mansion permanently.
- **Play in consistent sessions.** Currency accumulates faster across regular short sessions than one long weekend binge.

**Do you get a free house in Roblox High School 2?**

Community documentation states that new players receive a Simple House at no cost when they first join the game. **What is the most expensive house in Roblox High School 2?**

By the community-reported price list, the Mansion at 7,500 gems is the most expensive property overall. Among coin-priced homes, the Ski Lodge at 50,000 coins is the top tier.

**Are there more houses in Roblox High School 2 than the eight listed?**

The documented list contains eight properties: Simple House, Cozy Cabin, Bungalow, Ranch House, 2 Story House, Modern 2 Story House, Mansion, and Ski Lodge. **Which Roblox High School 2 houses should a new player buy first?**

Start with the Cozy Cabin at 1,500 coins. It's cheap enough to afford quickly, gives you an immediate upgrade over the free starter, and doesn't delay your savings for the Bungalow or Ranch House. Save the big purchases — the 2 Story House, Ski Lodge, and gem-tier properties — for after you've built a stable income from classes and jobs.

## Final Thoughts

The housing market in Roblox High School 2 rewards planning more than luck. Six of the eight homes run on coins, which means free-to-play players can climb the entire coin ladder with patience.
